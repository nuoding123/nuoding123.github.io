💘 Valentine's Pac-Man 🌹

Eat dots, avoid ghosts, grab roses for heart power-ups!

Score: 0
Lives: 3
Power-Up: --
Status: Ready

Controls: Arrow Keys or WASD to move

🟡 Pac-Man
👻 Ghosts
● Dots (Pellets)
🌹 Rose Power-Up
💕 Heart Projectiles

How to Play:

  • Navigate Pac-Man through the maze using arrow keys or WASD
  • Eat all the dots to complete the level
  • Avoid the ghosts - they'll chase you!
  • 🌹 Grab the rose to enter power-up mode
  • When powered up, you'll shoot 💕 hearts in your direction
  • Hearts destroy ghosts they hit
  • Power-up lasts for 8 seconds
  • You have 3 lives - don't lose them all!
