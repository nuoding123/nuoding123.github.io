How to Play - Classic Pac-Man with Valentine's Theme:

  1. Objective: Navigate Pac-Man through the maze to eat all the dots (pellets)
  2. Movement: Use arrow keys or WASD to move Pac-Man
  3. Eat Dots: Collect all dots in the maze to get points (+10 each)
  4. Avoid Ghosts: Four intelligent ghosts patrol the maze and chase you - each collision costs a life!
  5. Rose Power-Up 🌹: Randomly appears in the maze - grab it to enter power-up mode (+50 points)
  6. Heart Projectiles 💕: When powered up, continuously shoot hearts in your facing direction
  7. Destroy Ghosts: Hearts eliminate any ghost they hit (+200 points per ghost)
  8. Power Duration: Power-up lasts 8 seconds - use it wisely!
  9. Lives System: You have 3 lives. When all lives are lost, the game ends.
  10. Win Condition: Eat all dots to complete the level!
